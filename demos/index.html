Three ways to use one flash drive

Pick live ISOs for a toolkit stick, installed Linux systems for a thin daily-driver pool, or both when the stick is large enough. Windows always sees a branded volume so the drive is not a mystery.

Live ISOs

Big exFAT drop zone. Copy ISO files in Explorer, boot, pick from the menu.

Installed OSes

Small service exFAT plus a Btrfs pool of subvolumes that share free space.

Both

Sized ISO partition and install pool together. Two sticks are fine when space is tight.
